Our Story

From Degree to
Dough & Delicacies

Graduate Momo Wala was born from a simple observation: Bhilai lacked truly authentic, thin-skinned momos filled with fresh ingredients. Our founder, a local graduate, decided to trade the corporate path for the kitchen to perfect the art of the Himalayan dumpling.

We don't just sell momos; we sell a commitment to hygiene, quality, and flavor. Every piece is hand-folded, and every sauce is made from scratch every morning.
