The Spirit of Chaika Adda

Located in the heart of Bhilai, Chaika Adda isn't just a restaurant; it's a community hub. We started with a simple vision: to create a space where students can study, professionals can brainstorm, and friends can laugh—all over the perfect cup of ginger-infused tea.

Our ingredients are sourced locally, our recipes are generational secrets, and our vibe is unmatched in the city.
