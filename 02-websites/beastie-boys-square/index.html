COMMON INQUIRIES

What is Beastie Boys Square?
It is the officially designated intersection of Ludlow and Rivington Streets in Manhattan, New York, named in honor of the legendary hip-hop trio who immortalized the location on their iconic album art.
How can I support local businesses here?
Visit the many independent retailers, eateries, and galleries surrounding the square. Every purchase helps sustain the unique cultural fabric of the Lower East Side.
