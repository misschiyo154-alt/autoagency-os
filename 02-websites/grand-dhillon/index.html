Our Story

Serving Happiness Since 1998

Grand Dhillon has been Bhilai's landmark for gourmet food and exceptional hospitality for over two decades. What started as a passion for authentic flavors has evolved into the city's most beloved multi-cuisine destination.

Our master chefs curate each dish using farm-fresh ingredients and secret spice blends passed down through generations, ensuring every bite is a celebration.
