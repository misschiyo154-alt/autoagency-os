The Secret Behind Our Kitchen

Dashmesh Dhaba isn't just a restaurant; it's a celebration of Punjabi heritage in Bhilai. For over two decades, we've remained committed to the authentic dhaba experience—charcoal-fired tandoors, farm-fresh ingredients, and our signature secret spice blends.

Whether you're looking for a hearty family dinner or a quick satisfying lunch, we bring the warmth of North Indian hospitality to every plate we serve.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes, we deliver across Bhilai and nearby sectors. You can call us directly or find us on major food delivery apps.

Are there vegetarian-only options?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated wide range of pure vegetarian dishes prepared with separate utensils.

Do I need to book a table for weekends?

Weekends are busy! While we accept walk-ins, we highly recommend calling ahead for table reservations for groups of 4 or more.
