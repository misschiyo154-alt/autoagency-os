Our Story

Elevating Bhilai's Culinary Landscape Since 2010

Grand Dhillon isn't just a restaurant; it's a sanctuary for food connoisseurs. We pride ourselves on sourcing the freshest local ingredients to create authentic North Indian, Continental, and Oriental masterpieces.
