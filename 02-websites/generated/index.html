Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes! We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Our office will even handle the filing of your claims for you to ensure you maximize your benefits.

Are you accepting new patients?

Absolutely! We are always happy to welcome new members to our dental family. We have a special $99 cleaning and exam offer for new patients.

Do you offer emergency dental services?

Yes, we prioritize dental emergencies. If you are in pain or have a broken tooth, call us immediately and we will do our best to see you today.
