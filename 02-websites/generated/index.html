Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance PPO plans. We also offer internal payment plans and third-party financing through CareCredit.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital x-rays, oral cancer screening, and a consultation with Dr. Mitchell to discuss your goals.

How often should I have a dental check-up?

We generally recommend a cleaning and exam every six months to prevent plaque buildup and catch issues early.
