Our Story

Why "Graduate" Momo Wala?

It started with a simple vision: to bring a higher standard of street food to the heart of Bhilai. Founded by a passionate graduate who believed that street food deserves gourmet quality.

We don't just sell momos; we craft experiences. Using only farm-fresh ingredients and our secret signature spicy chutney, we’ve redefined the local snack culture.

  • Locally Sourced Ingredients
  • No MSG or Artificial Preservatives
