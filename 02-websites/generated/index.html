Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes! We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Our team will handle all the paperwork and help you maximize your benefits.

Are you accepting new patients?

Absolutely! We love welcoming new patients to our Dallas clinic. We have special introductory offers for new patient exams and cleanings.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We generally recommend a professional cleaning and exam every six months to prevent major issues and maintain optimal oral health.
