Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring for my first consultation?

Please bring any previous medical records, current medications, and a valid form of identification to help our doctors understand your medical history.

Do I need an appointment before visiting?

While walk-ins are seen based on availability, we recommend contacting us in advance to schedule an appointment for a shorter waiting time.

Are visiting hours restricted?

Yes, to ensure the rest and recovery of our patients, we maintain specific visiting hours. Please check with the front desk for current timings.

Is there parking available?

Public parking is generally available in the vicinity of the nursing home at Sindhi Colony.
