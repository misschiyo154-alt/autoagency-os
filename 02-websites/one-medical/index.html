Common Inquiries

What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring a valid photo ID, your current insurance information, and a list of any medications or supplements you are currently taking. Arriving 10 minutes early allows for a smooth check-in process.
Do you accept major insurance providers?
We work with a broad range of major insurance networks in New York. Please contact our administrative office to confirm your specific plan's coverage.
How do I access my medical records?
Patients can securely access their records, lab results, and visit summaries through our patient portal at any time.
